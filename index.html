About the studio

Clean floor. Serious equipment.

Legacy Fitness Studio is built around a simple idea: a well-lit, well-organised space where the equipment does the talking. Mirrored walls, rubber flooring and a clear layout keep the floor easy to move through, whether you train with machines, dumbbells or a barbell. We keep the space clean and hygienic, so you can focus on your workout.
